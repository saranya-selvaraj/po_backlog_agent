# \# Food Scoring App — Core Policy Document

# 

# \## 1. Ultra-Processing Classification Policy

# 

# The platform must provide clear and consistent information about the \*\*level of ultra-processing\*\* associated with food products. Ultra-processing levels must be determined using an agreed and documented methodology based on reliable product and ingredient information. The classification should be applied consistently across products and updated when relevant product information changes.

# 

# \## 2. Ultra-Processing Data Accuracy Policy

# 

# Information used to determine a product's ultra-processing level must be sourced from \*\*reliable and traceable data sources\*\*. Where information is incomplete, unavailable, or uncertain, the platform must avoid presenting potentially misleading information and should clearly indicate when a classification may require further verification. Data quality should be periodically reviewed to maintain the reliability of ultra-processing information.

# 

# \## 3. Ultra-Processing Transparency Policy

# 

# Customers must be able to understand what the \*\*ultra-processing level means\*\* and how it relates to the information presented about a food product. The platform should communicate this information in clear, non-technical language and avoid presenting the classification as a definitive statement about whether a food is inherently "good" or "bad". The purpose is to support informed customer decisions rather than prescribe them.

# 

# \## 4. Personalisation and Responsible Recommendations Policy

# 

# Where customer preferences, dietary requirements, or health goals are collected, this information must only be used to provide relevant and responsible personalised insights. Personalised information must not override the underlying food data or create misleading interpretations of a product's overall score.

# 

# \## 5. Customer Data Retention Policy

# 

# Customer information must only be retained for as long as it is required for the purposes for which it was collected or where there is a legitimate business or legal requirement to retain it. Personal data should be securely stored, appropriately protected, and deleted or anonymised when it is no longer required.

# 

# \## 6. Product Information Governance Policy

# 

# Changes to food scores, ultra-processing levels, product information, or scoring methodologies must follow an appropriate governance and review process. Significant changes should be traceable, with clear ownership and an audit history to support accountability, consistency, and customer trust.
